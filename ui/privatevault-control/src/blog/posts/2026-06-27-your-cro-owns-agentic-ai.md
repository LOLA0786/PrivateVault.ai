---
title: Your Chief Risk Officer Owns Agentic AI, Not Your CISO
date: 2026-06-27
excerpt: The crowded agent-security market sells to the SOC. The person who actually answers for an automated decision sits somewhere else.
---

Look at the agent security market and almost every product is aimed at the same person: the CISO. Identity, least-privilege, prompt-injection defense, runtime firewalls. That work matters. It secures the agent.

It does not prove the decision.

The executive who has to answer for an automated credit or claims decision is not the CISO. It is the Chief Risk Officer and the Head of Compliance. They are the ones who sit across from a regulator and explain why a pattern of denials is defensible. Their question is not whether the agent was authenticated. Their question is whether each decision stayed inside policy, and whether they can show it.

This is why so much agent governance misses regulated industries. It is built by security people for security people, and it answers the security question well. But the buyer with the real liability sits in the risk and compliance function, and that buyer needs a different thing: evidence that an auditor and a regulator will accept.

If you are building or buying governance for regulated AI and your only champion is in the security org, you are talking to the wrong floor of the building.
